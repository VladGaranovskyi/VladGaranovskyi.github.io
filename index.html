My first exposure to IT came in sixth grade, when my computer could not run CS:GO. I tried freeing memory, troubleshooting a grinding hard drive, changing system files, improving the network, and eventually upgrading the hardware. Solving that problem made me fall in love with the underlying process of thinking.

That curiosity moved from fixing computers to understanding how to design them. It led me into programming, then hardware and embedded systems, and helped shape my strongest qualities: resourcefulness, adaptability, versatility, and a willingness to learn by building.
